# Not Found Page Overrides

> **Route:** `404`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Use a minimal branded recovery state centered in the content area, with a calm headline and one sentence of guidance.
- Offer direct links to the shop and home page; avoid decorative illustration or oversized empty-state cards.

## Interaction

- Recovery links must be immediately visible and keyboard accessible.
