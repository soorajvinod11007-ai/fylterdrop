# About Page Overrides

> **Route:** `/about`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Tell the brand story as a short editorial sequence: point of view, curation principles, and how direct service works.
- Pair considered copy with a small number of authentic lifestyle/product images; avoid generic feature-card grids.
- Use a readable text measure around 680px and finish with a relevant shop or contact action.

## Interaction

- Keep motion limited to gentle image or opacity transitions; the story must read clearly without animation.
