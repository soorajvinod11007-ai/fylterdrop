# Product Detail Overrides

> **Route:** `/products/[slug]`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Use a two-column product composition on desktop: a generous image gallery and a stable information/order column.
- On mobile, place the gallery before title, price, variant selection, and order action.
- Prioritize product identity, current price, variants, availability, specifications, delivery, and support in that order.
- Keep related products visually secondary and use the same image ratio as the shop grid.

## Interaction

- Variant selection, quantity, gallery controls, and WhatsApp ordering remain keyboard accessible and explicitly labelled.
- Avoid AR, WebGL, rotation, or other interactions not supported by the current product data.
