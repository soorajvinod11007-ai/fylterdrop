# FAQ Page Overrides

> **Route:** `/faq`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Open with a concise title and help-oriented introduction, followed by a readable single-column question list.
- Group questions by ordering, products, payment, shipping, and aftercare when content supports those groups.
- End with a direct contact option for questions not answered on the page.

## Interaction

- Use native buttons for expandable questions with exposed expanded state and keyboard operation.
- Keep answers in the document reading order and avoid motion that delays access to the content.
