# Contact Page Overrides

> **Route:** `/contact`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Present contact choices and the inquiry form as a balanced two-column concierge layout on desktop.
- Give form labels, input borders, error messages, and submit state clear contrast on warm neutral surfaces.
- Include only verified contact details, operating hours, and social links.

## Interaction

- Keep inline validation, focus order, and confirmation/error feedback explicit.
- WhatsApp and Instagram are secondary contact options, not competing primary submit actions.
