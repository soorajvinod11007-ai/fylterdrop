# Shop Page Overrides

> **Route:** `/shop`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Treat this as a product-finding workspace, not a campaign landing page.
- Keep the title, search, category filters, sort controls, result count, and product grid in a clear hierarchy.
- Use a responsive four-column product grid on wide screens, two columns on phones, and consistent image ratios.
- Keep product image, category, name, price, availability, and direct-order action legible without hover.

## Interaction

- Filters and sorting must retain their current query/state behavior and have visible selected and focus states.
- Keep card actions stable and accessible; avoid tilt, scale, and hover-only product information.
