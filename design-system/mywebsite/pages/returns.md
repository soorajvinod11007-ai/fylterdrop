# Returns Page Overrides

> **Route:** `/returns`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Use the same legal-information template as shipping and other customer policies.
- Keep eligibility, request steps, exclusions, and refund handling in distinct numbered sections.
- Emphasize important deadlines and conditions with text hierarchy, never color alone.

## Interaction

- Link the customer to the relevant contact channel without obscuring policy details.
- Keep the full policy available in normal document flow and readable on narrow screens.
