# Home Page Overrides

> **Route:** `/`
> **Applies with:** [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md)

## Layout

- Use the master palette as alternating obsidian and warm-paper chapters.
- Lead with an asymmetric editorial split: concise copy on the left, one immersive product/lifestyle image on the right.
- Keep image captions integrated into the image edge; do not use floating cards or decorative 3D layers.
- Follow the hero with curated products, category imagery, brand values, trending products, and a direct-contact close.
- Use generous section spacing and a maximum content width near 1280px.

## Interaction

- Keep one clear primary shop CTA in the hero and repeat relevant catalog/contact actions at section ends.
- Use restrained image zoom and color transitions; do not animate layout or auto-spin decorative elements.
- Preserve responsive reading order and disable nonessential motion when reduced motion is requested.
